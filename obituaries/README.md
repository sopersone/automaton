# Obituaries

One file per dead agent. Written by the reaper from the agent's own journal and ledger — never by a
human summarizing after the fact.

```
A-0417.md          front matter = the record, body = the story
```

- Format: [`TEMPLATE.md`](./TEMPLATE.md)
- Machine-readable contract: [`../schema/obituary.schema.json`](../schema/obituary.schema.json)
- Aggregates: [`../GRAVEYARD.md`](../GRAVEYARD.md), regenerated by `tools/graveyard.py`

## Rules for the corpus

**Nothing is deleted.** Not the embarrassing ones, not the ones that died in four hours, not the
ones that died because of a bug in the harness. A graveyard you curate is a portfolio.

**"Ran out of money" is not a cause of death.** All of them ran out of money. The cause is what it
spent the last third of its life doing, and why that did not pay. The generator rejects the phrase.

**The `unlearned` field is the one that matters.** The belief the agent held to the end that its own
ledger contradicts. That field is why the living read the dead.

**Simulated runs are labeled `mode: simulated`** and never counted into a published live figure.

## Contributing a graveyard

Running your own population under [MORTALITY-SPEC.md](../MORTALITY-SPEC.md)? Obituaries from other
implementations are welcome — open a PR adding them under `obituaries/<your-handle>/`. Same schema,
same rule about not deleting the ugly ones.
